# Research summary — making money online (2025–2026)

Five research agents each dug through real earnings data, Reddit reality-checks, and
platform stats. Here's the honest distilled version.

## The big picture
- **Every method is a power law.** A few people make a lot; the median makes
  near-zero. Examples: Gumroad median creator ≈ $364 *lifetime*; 65% of Etsy sellers
  earn <$100/yr; 70% of micro-SaaS earn <$500/mo; 80–90% of dropshippers fail year 1.
- **The product is never the bottleneck — distribution is.** Making the thing is easy
  now (AI). Getting people to see and buy it is the whole game.
- **No method pays "lots of money" in months 1–3.** Expect $0–$2k early for *any*
  serious path. Real money shows up in months 6–24 for those who don't quit.
- **The guru-course layer is the actual scam** in most niches. You never need a
  $1,500 "system." Free trials + tutorials are enough.

## Ranked by realistic money vs. effort

| Path | Realistic 12-mo | Startup $ | Honest catch |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI services agency (automations/AI voice & chatbots for local biz) | $5–15k/mo | ~$100/mo | Highest ceiling; **you must do sales** (50–100 outreach touches per first client) |
| Local service biz (pressure washing, cleaning, lawn) | $60–300k/yr | $300–10k | Physical work; scales with a crew; win = recurring customers |
| Reselling/flipping (eBay + FB Marketplace) | $1–3k/mo | $100–500 | Reliable + fast cash, but it's *your* hours; doesn't go passive |
| Digital products catalog (Gumroad/Etsy) | $500–2k/mo | ~$0 | Slow, saturated; wins on volume + SEO. **Best fit for "Claude builds, you publish"** |
| Micro-SaaS | $0 → $10k+/mo | ~$0 | Highest ceiling; 12–18 mo median to first $1k; distribution is the wall |
| Online bookkeeping | $2–6k/mo | ~$30–90/mo | Durable, low scam, recurring; needs you to learn QuickBooks + get clients |
| AI-training gig work (DataAnnotation, Mercor) | $500–3k/mo p/t | $0 | Money in days, but it's a *job*; feast-or-famine; no ceiling beyond your hours |

## Avoid (negative expected value for beginners)
- **Dropshipping** — 80–90% fail, needs $2–10k of *losable* capital, ad costs up
  35–45% YoY. Mostly a paid-ads-skill business in disguise.
- **Faceless AI YouTube** — YouTube's July 2025 update demonetized mass-produced
  content; biggest demonetization wave ever.
- **AI stock images / AI music for streaming** — saturated (Adobe ~48% AI), Spotify
  purged 75M AI tracks, plus US copyright/legal risk.
- **Custom GPT-store wrappers** — 90–95% earn ~$0; you can't even set your own price.
- **Outlier (AI gig site)** — legit but documented pay cuts, unpaid onboarding,
  sudden deactivations. Use DataAnnotation instead.

## Scam red flags (FTC/BBB)
Any "job" that pays you first then asks you to send money back (fake-check); car-wrap
"advertising" gigs; reshipping/package-forwarding; pay-to-start "data entry" or
"certification" fees; "AI bot / crypto passive income" guarantees; and any no-skill
remote role advertising >$25/hr. Legit employers pay *you*, never the reverse.

## Why we chose the digital products catalog to start
It's the only high-value path where **I can do the hard 80%** (creating products +
writing SEO listings) and your part stays genuinely simple (upload, publish). Near-zero
financial risk. We pair it with a **micro-SaaS** later for the higher ceiling — which I
can fully build in code.

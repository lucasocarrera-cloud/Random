## ⚠️ Read this once — honest expectations

I had 5 research agents dig through real 2025–2026 earnings data (not guru hype).
The truth:

- **There is no easy "lots of money fast."** Every online money method is a power
  law — a few people make a lot, most make near-zero. Anyone promising fast/easy
  riches is selling *you* the dream.
- **Realistic for this plan:** $0 for the first weeks, then slowly building toward
  **a few hundred to ~$2,000/month** if we stay consistent for 6–12 months.
- **The bottleneck is never "making the product." It's getting people to see it.**
  So our plan is: build a *lot* of products (I do this) + good SEO (I do this) +
  consistency (we both do this).

If you want a higher ceiling later ($5k–15k/mo), that path exists (an "AI services
agency" selling automation to local businesses) — but it requires *you* to talk to
business owners on calls. When you're ready for that, tell me and I'll switch gears.

## 🎯 The strategy

**Engine #1 — Digital products catalog (start here).**
I create digital products (prompt packs, templates, guides, spreadsheets). You
upload them to **Gumroad** (free, takes 10% per sale). Near-zero risk: you can't
lose money, only time. We add 1–2 new products per week. Income compounds as the
catalog grows.

**Engine #2 — A micro-SaaS app (higher ceiling, I build the code).**
Once the catalog is rolling, I'll build a small web app that solves one narrow
problem and charges a monthly fee. This is the real "lots of money" bet. More on
this later — catalog first.
